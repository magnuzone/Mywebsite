Service Exclusions

To ensure fast mobile dispatch times across the Omaha Metro, please note we DO NOT offer the following services or work on the following vehicles:

  • Computer Flashing / Reprogramming
  • Key Making / Locksmithing
  • Deep Electrical Diagnostics or Wiring
  • A/C Repair or Recharging
  • Vehicle Alignments
  • Internal Engine or Transmission Rebuilds
  • European Luxury (BMW, Audi, Volvo, Mercedes-Benz)
  • Diesels / Heavy-Duty Commercial
  • Electric Vehicles (Tesla, Rivian, etc.)
  • Hybrid Vehicles

Disclaimer: For safety, environmental, and local zoning reasons, not all major automotive repairs can be safely or legally completed in a residential driveway or public parking lot. If a job is deemed unsafe for a mobile environment during the diagnostic process, we will advise you immediately on the best next steps.
